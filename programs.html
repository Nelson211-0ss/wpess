Flagship Program

Sport for Peace

In March 2025, WPESS organized a football tournament held over two days in Obbo Payam, Magwi County, one of the areas hardest hit by conflict, where insecurity, mistrust, and displacement had strained relationships between neighboring communities.

The tournament brought together 325 players from six bomas (Labato, Lowudo, Alia, Lerwa, Cama, and Licari), including 150 women playing alongside 150 men, with 25 community leaders taking part and more than 1,000 spectators attending. Matches were organized so women could take part in a supportive environment where they felt safe, motivated, and empowered.

Partners: GIZ South Sudan, the Community-Driven Rural Development (CDRD) project, the German Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ), the Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands, and the Magwi County Local Football Association.

Our Programmes

Where else we work

Alongside Sport for Peace, WPESS runs programmes across Magwi County covering protection, education, and community wellbeing.

Sexual & Gender-Based Violence (SGBV) Support

A women's centre in Magwi supporting SGBV survivors, with community awareness campaigns, individual and group counselling, and stronger referral and follow-up services.

Magwi, July 2022 Funded by UNHCR

Peacebuilding & Human Rights

Community dialogues across six payams of Magwi County, facilitating reconciliation between victims, improving social cohesion, and coordinating service providers for evidence-based referrals.

Magwi, since Sept 2022 Funded by UNHCR

Child Protection

Improved planning and coordination for orphans and vulnerable children (OVC), youth, and caregivers, with better service delivery models and referral systems.

Magwi, ongoing Volunteer-run

Enhanced Girl Child Education

Over 100 community dialogue meetings on the value of girl-child education, sensitisation across 30 schools, and support to keep girls in school.

2,400 OVC supported 5,520 given psychosocial support
