Who We Are

Grassroots peacebuilding, led by the community it serves

WPESS started in 2019 as a small community group of women responding to the internal displacement, poverty, and poor health caused by South Sudan's civil conflict. Today we work across six payams of Magwi County, alongside partners including UNHCR and GIZ.

Featured Program · Sport for Peace

A playing field that became common ground

In March 2025, WPESS organized a two-day football tournament in Obbo Payam, Magwi County, one of the areas hardest hit by conflict. 325 players from six bomas (Labato, Lowudo, Alia, Lerwa, Cama, and Licari) took part, with 150 women playing alongside 150 men and 25 community leaders.
