Grassroots peacebuilding, led by the community it serves

WPESS empowers vulnerable women and children through education, protection, and dialogue — turning displacement and mistrust into resilience and shared ground.

Vision & Mission

"Every woman, every child, full potential."

Our vision: a South Sudan where every woman and child is guaranteed full rights and attains their full potential for community wellness.

Our mission: to empower vulnerable children and women through education, nutrition, child protection and peacebuilding, fostering resilience and community sustainability.

WPESS started in 2019 as a small community group of women responding to the internal displacement, poverty, and poor health caused by South Sudan's civil conflict. Since then we've grown to work across six payams of Magwi County, earning the respect of local communities and partners including UNHCR and GIZ.

We work through three pillars: dialogue between community and traditional leaders, sport and shared events that rebuild everyday trust, and women's leadership that gives women real standing in public life.

"We don't bring peace from outside — we help communities find it in themselves."

Our Team

Leadership

WPESS is guided by an Executive Director, an elected Board of Trustees, and a multi-skilled management team.

Executive Director

Anyek Rose John Oling

Leads WPESS's strategy and day-to-day operations, and has guided the organization's growth since its founding in 2019.

Governance Body

Board of Trustees

WPESS's top governance body: a diverse group of elected members and volunteers. The current Board Chair is a qualified teacher with extensive experience in girl-child education.

Operations Team

Management & Technical Team

A multi-skilled team covering health, finance, monitoring & evaluation, human resources, and programming, steering WPESS toward its mission.
