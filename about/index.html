About WPESS

Grassroots peacebuilding led by and for the communities of South Sudan.

Vision & Mission

"Every woman, every child, full potential."

Our vision: a South Sudan where every woman and child is guaranteed full rights and attains their full potential for community wellness.

Our mission: to empower vulnerable children and women through education, nutrition, child protection and peacebuilding, fostering resilience and community sustainability.

WPESS started in 2019 as a small community group of women responding to the internal displacement, poverty, and poor health caused by South Sudan's civil conflict. Since then we've grown to work across six payams of Magwi County, earning the respect of local communities and partners including UNHCR and GIZ.

Our Approach

Three pillars of our work

Dialogue

Facilitated conversations between community and traditional leaders to address the roots of local conflict.

Sport & Community Bonding

Tournaments and shared events that give neighboring communities a reason to stand side by side again.

Women's Leadership

Creating safe, supported space for women to take part in public and community life, not just as bystanders to peace.

Our Story

Peace, in practice: Magwi County

In March 2025, WPESS organized a "Sport for Peace" football tournament held over two days in Obbo Payam, Magwi County, one of the areas hardest hit by conflict, marked by insecurity, mistrust, and displacement. 325 players from six neighboring bomas took part, including 150 women, alongside 25 community leaders and more than 1,000 spectators.

It's a small, concrete example of how we work: bring divided communities together around something everyone can share, give women equal footing to participate, and let trust rebuild from there. The tournament was supported by GIZ South Sudan through the Community-Driven Rural Development (CDRD) project, funded by the German Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ) and the Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands.
